\footnotesize
Looks are the planned cumulative sample sizes, the last being the maximum sample size $N$. The tuning parameters maximize the power subject to a type I error rate of at most $\alpha$, with ties broken by the expected sample size under $p_0$ and then by the order of the grid, over $\lambda$ from 0 to 1 and $\gamma$ from 0.01 to 1 in steps of 0.01 and $\eta$ from 0.1 to 3 in steps of 0.1. BOP2-FE-1 and BOP2-FE-2 use the power and the O'Brien-Fleming type efficacy cutoffs. At an interim analysis the trial stops for futility when the number of responses is at most the futility boundary and for efficacy when it is at least the efficacy boundary; at the final analysis the null hypothesis is rejected when the number of responses is at least the efficacy boundary. A dash means no efficacy stopping. The type I error rate is computed with the futility rule shown; the power and $E_0(N)$, the expected sample size under $p_0$, are computed with the futility boundaries applied. When several grid points give the same boundaries, the one with the smallest $\lambda$, then the smallest $\gamma$ and $\eta$, is shown (Section S2.8). Type I error rates printed as the nominal level do not exceed it before rounding. All values are exact.
