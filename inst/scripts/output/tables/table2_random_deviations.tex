\footnotesize
Settings and planned designs are given in Table~\ref{tab:planned}. Each planned interim sample size was shifted by an integer drawn uniformly from $-2$ to $2$ and the maximum sample size by one drawn from $-4$ to $4$, independently of the data; the same schedules were used for all designs of a setting. Naive: the planned probability cutoffs evaluated at the actual sample sizes. Proposed: boundaries from the error spending function implied by the planned design, with the efficacy boundary not below the planned final posterior cutoff. Mean and Max are taken over schedules, and $>\alpha$ is the percentage of schedules with a type I error rate above the nominal level. Planned is the power of the planned design on the planned schedule; Naive and Proposed are averaged over schedules. Proposed $-$ oracle is the mean difference in power between the proposed boundaries and the design calibrated on the actual schedule as if it had been known in advance, over the first 100 schedules (binding futility only). All operating characteristics are exact.
